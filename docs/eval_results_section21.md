## Metric Reconciliation (§21 code changes)

Three metrics now clearly separated:

| Metric | Formula | Used For |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **Reporting composite** | `(0.38×MAE_1m + 0.104×MAE_3m + 0.40×MAE_6m + 0.255×MAE_12m) / 1.139` per-horizon simple avg | Blog table, `print_composite_table()` in `evaluate_model.py` |
| **Optuna mae objective** | `compute_composite_metric()` — per-sample weighted MAE with series/regime/magnitude weights, Optuna-searched horizon weights | `tune_params --objective mae` |
| **Optuna conditional objective** | `compute_conditional_objective()` — `0.3×condMAE + 0.3×(1-F1)×200 + 0.2×6mKeyMAE + 0.2×overallMAE` | `tune_params --objective conditional` (preferred) |

The reporting composite is transparent and reproducible. The optimization objectives are
intentionally different — they target user-centric quality (movement detection, long-horizon
accuracy on key EB-2/3 series), not raw MAE averaging.
