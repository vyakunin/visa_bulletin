Final-action dates move in lumpy jumps — most months they don't move at all — so a "no movement next month" call is usually correct, and that's where many of these land. The harder, more useful test is the multi-month trajectory: on a 2016–2026 walk-forward backtest the model beats the naive "assume no change" baseline at the 6-month horizon and calls the direction of movement far more often than that baseline. We only score ourselves on the series we publicly predict (EB-1/2/3 China, India, and Rest of World); policy-driven categories (EB-4/EB-5) are excluded because we don't forecast them.
