Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information visa-bulletin.us ("we," "us," or "the site") collects when you visit, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

visa-bulletin.us is an independent, editorial data site covering U.S. Visa Bulletin movement, employment-based green-card timelines, and publicly filed labor and wage data. We are not affiliated with the U.S. Department of State, USCIS, the Department of Labor, or any government agency. You can reach us at privacy@visa-bulletin.us.

Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask you for personal information to read the site. We collect only:

  • Aggregate, non-identifying usage statistics — pages viewed, referrer, country, browser, and device type — to understand traffic and improve content.
  • Standard server logs — IP address, request time, and user-agent — retained briefly for security, abuse prevention, and debugging.
  • Information you choose to send us — for example, the contents of an email you send to us.

Analytics

We use two analytics tools:

  • GoatCounter — a privacy-friendly analytics service that does not use tracking cookies and does not collect personal data. See the GoatCounter privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) — provided by Google, which uses cookies and similar technologies to measure how visitors use the site. IP addresses are anonymized where supported. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising

We display advertising to support the site, including through Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.eu (EU).

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. We only feature products and services we consider relevant to our audience, and affiliate relationships do not influence our editorial data or analysis. This disclosure is provided in accordance with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. On this site they are used by GA4 and by advertising partners as described above. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent; some parts of the site may not function as intended if you disable them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information about you, and to object to certain processing. Because we do not maintain user accounts or directly collected personal profiles, most data we hold is non-identifying analytics. To make a request or ask a question, email privacy@visa-bulletin.us.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party links

Our content links to government and third-party sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites; this policy applies only to visa-bulletin.us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@visa-bulletin.us.
