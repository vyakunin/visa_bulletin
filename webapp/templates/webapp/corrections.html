Corrections & Updates Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

visa-bulletin.us covers government immigration and wage data that people use to make real decisions. Getting it right matters, and when we get something wrong we want to fix it quickly and visibly. This page explains how.

How we keep data current

  • Visa Bulletin cut-off dates are re-parsed automatically from the U.S. Department of State within about a day of each monthly release.
  • Employment and wage figures are refreshed from U.S. Department of Labor disclosure files as new quarterly data is published.
  • Forecasts re-run automatically whenever the underlying bulletin or demand data updates.

Reporting an error

If you find an incorrect date, figure, name, or statement — or a forecast that looks clearly broken — please tell us:

  • Email vyakunin@gmail.com with the page URL and what looks wrong, or
  • Open an issue on GitHub.

Please include the specific page and, where you can, the correct value and its source. That lets us verify against the official government record.

How we handle corrections

  • Data errors (a mis-parsed date, a wrong figure) are fixed at the source and flow to the live site on the next refresh. We aim to correct clear factual errors within a few days of confirming them.
  • Methodology or interpretation issues are reviewed and, where warranted, the affected pages and the methodology page are updated.
  • Dating. Policy and explainer pages carry a “last updated” date. Data pages reflect the most recent government release we have ingested.

What we don't do

We do not change or override official government figures. Where our reading of a bulletin differs from the source, the official Department of State Visa Bulletin always governs.
