How a second round works

USCIS selects more registrations than the {{ annual_cap|intcomma }} cap because not every selected registrant files a petition. When the petitions that do arrive will not fill the cap, it selects again from the registrations already submitted that March. There is nothing to re-submit and no second registration period: a registration that was not selected the first time stays in the pool for the rest of that cap season, and anyone newly selected is notified in their USCIS online account.

What this does and does not tell you about FY{{ season.cap_fy }}

It does not predict one. Whether USCIS runs another round for FY{{ season.cap_fy }} turns on how many selectees filed during the initial window — a count only USCIS holds, and one it acts on before publishing. What the table above gives is the base rate: how often an additional round has happened in the seasons we can measure, and how much of a season came through it when it did. Treat anyone quoting a probability for the current season as quoting a guess.
