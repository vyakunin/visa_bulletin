How the priority date calculator works

The U.S. State Department publishes a Visa Bulletin each month with two sets of cutoff dates per category and country. Your priority date is your place in line. You are current when your priority date is earlier than the cutoff:

  • Final Action Dates — when USCIS or the consulate can actually approve and issue the green card.
  • Dates for Filing — when you may submit the application (Form I-485 or DS-260). These are usually ahead of Final Action, so you can often file before approval.
  • A category shown as Current (C) has no backlog; Unavailable (U) means its numbers for the fiscal year are used up (it typically reopens around October 1).
