Selection is not a visa

Every rate above is a rate of selection. A selected registration earns the right to file the I-129 petition inside the filing window; the petition still has to be filed and approved, and a real share are denied. The FY2021 and FY2022 rates are also inflated by the extra selection rounds those seasons ran after too few selectees filed, so they overstate what a single draw was worth.

What the single yearly rate hides is what set the odds before FY2025: how many employers registered the same person. Conditioned on that, the one rate splits in two — the full decomposition, built from these same USCIS totals.
